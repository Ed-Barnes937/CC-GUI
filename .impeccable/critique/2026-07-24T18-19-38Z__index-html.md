## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored for this product in substance; generic in skeleton. Not category-interchangeable.**

The scaffolding is the familiar "1-2-3 cards under a centered hero" any SaaS uses, but the content is unmistakably this product: "isolated worktrees", "fresh branch + worktree", "always-on session to orchestrate the rest", "commander", "⌘K to jump or run a command", "Attach ⏎". Every color is a `var(--token)` / `color-mix()` (`style.css:3054-3194`) so the surface reskins with all 19 themes — it honors the 21-token contract instead of hardcoding a look. You could not drop this into a different product unchanged.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs --json index.html` returned `[]`, exit 0 — clean, zero findings, no false positives. The detector is markup-only and flagged nothing on the onboarding block or elsewhere in the page. Agreement with the design review: the token/theming discipline the detector would catch is genuinely clean here. The real issues (focus, semantics, affordance honesty) are behavioral/semantic and sit below the mechanical detector's resolution.

**Visual overlays:** none — native macOS WKWebView, no browser/screenshot driver and no dev server, so live-server injection and overlays are unavailable. All visual/contrast/responsive claims are read from markup + CSS, not rendered pixels; exact contrast ratios and clip thresholds are not measured.

**Philosophical tension (real but pre-authorized):** PRODUCT.md principle #2 is "expert density over onboarding" and #4 is "native, not web-in-a-frame", yet this is a 34px centered display headline over a radial accent gradient — the most marketing-hero moment in the app. DESIGN.md explicitly licenses Display type for "the first-run onboarding hero only", so it is the sanctioned exception. It leans slightly salesy ("Run your agents, side by side.") for an expert who just installed a dev tool, but it is purely state-driven (`onboardingActive()`, `main.ts:3164`) with no persisted "seen" flag — it never nags and yields the instant a project lands or a terminal attaches.

## Overall Impression

Content-authentic, token-clean, and it never nags — a genuinely good empty state for this audience. The single biggest gap is that the one screen most in need of the product's keyboard-first identity is the one screen that abandons it: no autofocus, no focus ring, and a keyboard glyph (⏎) advertised on a card that is disabled by default. The surface promises keyboard operability and then makes the primary path mouse-only.

## What's Working

1. **Cancel-to-path-input fallback** (`main.ts:3190-3198`). The primary action can't dead-end — a cancelled native picker routes to the manual sidebar path input. Respects user control and matches how experts actually work. Emotional high point of the surface.
2. **Card 3 disabled-when-unconfigured, by design** (`main.ts:3176-3181`). The Attach button is inert until the commander is actually configured, specifically so it can't fire `prepare_commander` into a raw error toast. Error prevention baked into render, not bolted on.
3. **Fully token-driven and product-specific** (`style.css:3054-3194`). No hardcoded color anywhere; accent/success/info icon tints via `color-mix()`. Detector-confirmed clean. Combined with the domain vocabulary and ⌘K/⏎ glyphs, it reads as this tool, not a template.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] No `:focus-visible` on CTAs + no autofocus on the primary action.**
- Why it matters: `.onboarding-cta` (`style.css:3152-3179`) defines no focus style, and `renderOnboarding()` (`main.ts:3168-3182`) never focuses anything. On the one activation surface, a keyboard-first product (principle #3) forces the mouse or blind Tabbing with no visible ring. Detector B confirmed no `:focus`/`:focus-visible` matches onboarding while other components (`.tb-pill.attention`, `#sessions`, settings inputs) do have rings.
- Fix: add `.onboarding-cta:focus-visible { outline:2px solid var(--accent); outline-offset:2px; }` and `#onboarding-add-project.focus()` when the hero shows.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P1] The "⏎" on "Attach" is a misleading affordance.**
- Why it matters: `index.html:73` promises Enter attaches the commander, but nothing autofocuses that button, it's `disabled` by default (`main.ts:3179`), and there's no global Enter binding — only a `click` handler (`main.ts:3202`). Enter fires only in the incidental case where the user already Tab-focused that usually-disabled button. Both assessments independently flagged this.
- Fix: move the ⏎ affordance to the primary CTA and autofocus it, or drop the glyph from a control that's disabled by default.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Keyboard hint is on the wrong card (hierarchy inversion).**
- Why it matters: the only per-action key affordance (⏎) sits on the tertiary, usually-disabled card 3, while the true primary path (card 1 "Choose folder…", `index.html:61`) has no key affordance and isn't focusable by default. The primary journey is effectively mouse-only on a keyboard-first product.
- Fix: card 1 gets Enter + autofocus; demote card 3's glyph.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P2] Silent success, raw-string failure on `add_project`** (`main.ts:3192-3194`).
- Why it matters: success = hero silently disappears (no confirmation beat); failure = `add project failed: ${err}` dumped verbatim (heuristics 1 and 9). A first-timer gets no "you're set", and an error exposes an internal string instead of a diagnosis.
- Fix: toast a plain "Added <name>" on success; map common errors ("not a git repo", "already added") to human language.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`

**[P2] No heading structure or ARIA on the first-run screen** (`index.html:49-79`).
- Why it matters: the title is a `<div class="onboarding-title">`, card titles are `<div>`s — zero `<h1>`/`<h2>`, no `role`, no `aria-*`, no `tabindex`, and the ＋ ❯ ◈ glyphs are decorative Unicode with no `aria-hidden`/label. The `#onboarding` container isn't a labeled region. A screen-reader user gets no landmark and hears ambiguous glyph characters. Detector B confirmed none present.
- Fix: promote the hero title to `<h1>`/`<h2>`, add `aria-hidden="true"` to icon glyphs, and label the region.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P3] The inert "After a project" span looks like a button** (`index.html:67`; `style.css:3174-3179`).
- Why it matters: it carries the same border, padding, and radius as the real outline CTA, inviting a dead click; it's a non-interactive `<span>` reusing `.onboarding-cta`.
- Fix: make inert placeholders visually non-pressable (drop the border/button padding, dim further) so only live CTAs read as clickable.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User):** Keyboard-first identity breaks here — primary CTA isn't focused, CTAs have no focus ring, the advertised ⏎ is on a disabled card, and ⌘K doesn't add a project. Also likely finds the marketing headline beneath the tool's tone. The persona this surface most disappoints.

**Jordan (First-Timer):** Barely in-audience by design. Reasonably guided, but "worktree"/"commander" may stay opaque, the "After a project" button-that-isn't confuses, a failed add shows a raw error string, and success has no "you're set" confirmation.

**Expert Internal Maintainer (project persona):** Fine once — state-driven, no nag — and appreciates the cancel→path-input fallback and the ⌘K pointer. Their one real gripe overlaps Alex's: the keyboard-first promise isn't honored on the single screen where a brand-new install has no muscle memory yet.

## Minor Observations

- **＋ is U+FF0B (fullwidth plus)** at `index.html:58` — different metrics from the normal-width ❯ and ◈ siblings. Use a regular `+`.
- **Fractional font sizes** (12.5px at `style.css:3146,3183`) — harmless but unusual; snap to 12/13.
- **`.onboarding-cards` has no `flex-wrap`** and cards have no `min-width`/`flex-basis` (`style.css:3092-3100`); at narrow window widths cards squeeze and copy can crowd/clip rather than wrap. No `@media` touches onboarding.
- **Radial accent gradient** (`style.css:3062-3066`) is the one place the shell doesn't recede — sanctioned, but the surface's most "web-in-a-frame" note.
- **Cross-cutting (not onboarding):** `src/main.ts` contains embedded NUL bytes (`main.ts:1624`, `main.ts:3459`, intentional composite-key separators) that make BSD `grep`/`rg` treat the file as binary and silently skip it — misleads any grep-based audit or CI. Not a visual defect; worth noting to maintainers.

## Questions to Consider

1. Given "expert density over onboarding", does this need a headline + sub + 3 cards + hint at all — or would a single focused "Point me at a repo" input with a ⌘K hint serve the actual audience better?
2. Card 2 is never actionable from this surface — is a permanently-inert "step" earning a third of the width, or would two live actions (Add project / Attach commander) plus one explanatory line be tighter and more honest?
3. Should the primary CTA fire on a bare Enter at first paint (autofocus), making the keyboard-first identity real on the one screen where a new user has no muscle memory?
